# Preliminary fabrication review — engineering HOLD

The member schedule and BOM are design-review quantities generated from the current parametric study. Tube lengths are centreline or straight-endpoint lengths; no cope, mitre, weld-gap, distortion allowance, or end preparation is released.

The selected fabrication concept uses a datum jig around the rear axle origin, two straight cabin sill rails, floor crossmembers, and a shared front/cabin lap. Tack and remeasure the primary rails before adding the diagonal and donor cradles. Balance weld sequence left/right to control distortion. Seal/cap open RHS ends only after drainage, inspection, and internal corrosion treatment are approved.

All GX100 and Probox brackets are proxy plates or measured-point placeholders. The bracket drilling HOLD schedule must be closed with physical donor scans, fastener/bushing data, supplier damper geometry, load cases, local bracket analysis, and tool-access review. Seat and belt anchors require a qualified occupant safety design. The rear cassette must remain removable without compromising the primary rails.

The fixture concept establishes X=0 rear axle and a level Z=0 ground datum, with crossmember stations at X=915, 1240, 1630, 2010, and 2526 mm. These are locating references for a review jig, not approved manufacturing tolerances.
